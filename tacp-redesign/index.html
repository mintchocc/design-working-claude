How each loan type works

The loan rule decides how much of a transfer becomes cash for Noble
Debt not enteredThe transfer counts as cash in full until debt figures are added in Projects & terms.
Bank release rateWhen units sell below the release rate, Noble tops up the gap before titles are freed.
All to debtTransfers repay the project loan first. No cash reaches Noble until it is cleared.
JV ownershipCash is split by ownership %. With no % set, the project counts as 100% Noble.
